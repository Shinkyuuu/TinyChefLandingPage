TinyChef ("TinyChef", "we", "us") is a social recipe app operated by Mimansa Tyagi, an individual, under the name SiCo Labs. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using TinyChef you agree to this policy.

**Contact:** sico.labs.admin@gmail.com

## 1. Who can use TinyChef

TinyChef is for people who are 13 or older. We ask you to confirm this when you sign up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

TinyChef is intended for users in the United States.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account information:** first and last name, email address, username, password (stored and verified by our authentication provider; we never see it in readable form), and optionally a phone number.
- **Profile information:** bio and profile picture.
- **Content you create:** recipes (titles, descriptions, ingredients, steps, tags, times, nutrition, dietary info), recipe photos, collections, and stories.
- **Photos and links you import:** if you use recipe import, the photo or web page text you provide.
- **Reports and messages to us:** content you report and anything you email us.

**Information created by your use of TinyChef**

- **Activity:** accounts you follow and who follows you, likes and dislikes, saves and collections, blocks, and notifications.
- **Settings:** privacy and display preferences. Some display preferences are stored only on your device.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Device and log data:** IP address, request time, device and app version, and similar technical data, recorded by our servers when the app contacts them.
- **Update data:** when the app checks for updates, our update provider receives basic device and app version information.
- **Analytics data:** how you use TinyChef (screens viewed, features used, recipes viewed or saved, taps and navigation), crash and performance data, device type, operating system, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and identifiers assigned to your device or app install.
- **Advertising data:** your device's advertising identifier (where you permit it), ad impressions and interactions, and information about your interests inferred from your use of TinyChef, collected by us and by our advertising partners.

**Where we get information.** Directly from you; automatically from your device and our servers as you use TinyChef; and from other users, who may tag, follow, block or report you or interact with your content. We do not buy information about you from third parties.

**Permissions on your device**

- **Camera:** only when you choose to take a photo for a recipe, a profile picture, or recipe import.
- **Photo library:** only to the photos you choose.

We do not collect your location or contacts.

## 3. How we use information

- To create and run your account and provide TinyChef's features (feed, search, follows, collections, notifications).
- To show your public content to other users and make it searchable.
- To keep TinyChef safe: detect and review spam, abuse and content that breaks our rules, handle reports, and enforce our Terms.
- To import recipes you ask us to import.
- To secure the service, prevent fraud, debug problems and maintain performance.
- To communicate with you about your account, safety, support, and changes to TinyChef.
- To improve TinyChef, including measuring how features are used, diagnosing crashes, and developing new features.
- To show advertising, measure how well it performs, and, where you allow it, personalize it.
- To personalize your experience, such as recommending recipes based on what you view, save and like.
- To comply with law and protect our rights.

We may also use personal information for purposes we describe to you when we collect it, or that you agree to.

## 4. What is public

- Your **username, name, bio, profile picture, follower and following counts, and public recipes** can be seen by anyone, and are indexed in our search.
- Your **likes and saves** are visible to other users by default. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.
- Recipes you mark **private** and your **drafts** are visible only to you.
- **Dislikes** are private and are not shown to the recipe's creator.

Content you make public may be copied or shared by others outside our control.

## 5. Who we share information with

We share information as described below. We may also share or make available limited information with advertising and analytics partners, and we may in the future share or license de-identified or aggregated data (data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you). We do not sell your account details, such as your email address, phone number or password, and we will tell you in this policy before we begin any sale or sharing of personal information that the law treats as such. We share it with:

**Service providers who process data for us**

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** hosting, database, file storage, authentication (Cognito), and image safety scanning (Rekognition).
- **Algolia:** search. Public recipe and profile data is sent to Algolia to power search.
- **OpenAI:** (a) to check text you post (for example recipes and bios) against our content rules, and (b) to read recipe photos or web page text when you use recipe import. Content you submit for these purposes is sent to OpenAI through its business API. Under OpenAI's current terms, API content is not used to train its models by default, and OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days to detect abuse, or longer where required by law. OpenAI's practices are governed by its own policies and may change.
- **Expo:** delivery of app updates.
- **Analytics providers:** usage and crash data.
- **Advertising partners:** advertising identifiers, device data, and ad interactions so they can serve and measure ads, and personalize them where you allow it. These partners have their own privacy policies and may combine this data with data from other apps and sites.

**Other users**, as described in section 4.

**Links and imported recipes.** TinyChef may link to, or let you import recipes from, websites we do not operate. Those sites have their own privacy practices and we are not responsible for them. Only import content you have the right to use.

**Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if we believe it is required by law or legal process, or necessary to protect the safety, rights or property of users, the public, or us, and to investigate violations of our Terms.

**Business transfers.** If TinyChef's operator is involved in a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or transfer of the app to a new legal entity (for example, when the app moves from an individual to a company), your information may be transferred, and we will tell you if it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 6. Content moderation

We use automated tools (image and text scanning) and human review to detect content that breaks our rules. Content may be hidden or removed, and accounts may be restricted or banned. When content is reported or flagged we keep a record of the case, including the content and the decision, so we can enforce our rules and handle appeals.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Edit** your profile and recipes in the app.
- **Visibility:** choose public or private for each recipe and your default, and hide your activity, in Settings.
- **Block and report** other users and content.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and removes your recipes, collections, likes, saves and follow relationships from our live systems and adjusts related counts.
- **Permissions:** you can turn off camera and photo access in your device settings.
- **Ask us:** email sico.labs.admin@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. We will respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need more time.

**US state privacy rights.** Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Texas and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it, to access and correct it, to delete it, to receive a portable copy, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, email sico.labs.admin@gmail.com; we may need to verify your identity, and you may use an authorized agent. If we deny a request you may appeal by replying to our response. Because we use advertising and analytics partners, some of that activity may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising or a "sale" under some state laws. You can opt out by turning off tracking for TinyChef in your iOS or Android settings, or by emailing us. We honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control where the law requires. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

**Withdrawing consent.** Where we rely on your consent (for example, camera and photo permissions or optional emails), you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings or by emailing us. This does not affect what we did before. If you withdraw consent, some features may stop working.

**Automated decisions.** We use automated tools to scan content and to rank your feed. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated means. If automated moderation hides or removes your content, you can ask for a human review by emailing us.

**Do Not Track.** The TinyChef app does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because they are a web-browser feature. See above for how to opt out of personalized advertising.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Account and content:** until you delete them.
- **After deletion:** removed from our live systems right away. Encrypted backups may persist for up to 35 days before being permanently purged.
- **Moderation and safety records:** we may keep records of reported or removed content and enforcement decisions for up to 2 years after the case is closed, and longer if needed to resolve a dispute or comply with law. These records may remain after you delete your account.
- **Notifications:** expire automatically.
- **Server logs:** technical logs from our servers (which may include your user ID, IP address, request details, and the web address of a recipe you import) are kept for as long as needed for security, debugging, fraud prevention and legal purposes, and may remain after you delete your account. We do not use them to rebuild your deleted profile.
- **Analytics and advertising data:** kept for as long as needed for those purposes, generally no more than 26 months, according to the settings of our providers.
- **Legal obligations:** we may keep information longer where the law requires.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our systems is limited. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider. No service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Choose a strong password and tell us right away about any unauthorized access.

## 10. International users

TinyChef is intended for the United States and your information is processed in the United States.
