## Contact us

Email [sico.labs.admin@gmail.com](mailto:sico.labs.admin@gmail.com). Tell us which device you use and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can.

## Report a recipe, story or profile

Open the recipe, story or profile in the app and use the report option. Our team will review it. Read how this works in the [community guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Block an account

Block someone from their profile. To review or undo blocks, go to Profile → Settings → Blocked Accounts.
