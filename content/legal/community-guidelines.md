## What to share

Share recipes, photos and stories that you made or have the right to post. Give credit when a recipe comes from someone else.

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, bullying, hate or threats toward other people.
- Sexual content, graphic violence or content that exploits or endangers children.
- Illegal content, or content that promotes dangerous or illegal activity.
- Spam, scams, misleading links or fake accounts.
- Content that infringes someone else's copyright or privacy.
- Dietary badges or recipe details you know to be false, especially allergy information.

## Report something

You can report a recipe, story or profile from the app. Reports go to our team, who will review them. If you believe content infringes your rights, see the copyright section of our [terms](/terms).

## Block someone

You can block any account. Manage the accounts you have blocked in Profile → Settings → Blocked Accounts.
