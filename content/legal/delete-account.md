You can delete your TinyChef account yourself, at any time.

## Delete from the app

1. Open TinyChef and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
2. Follow the prompts to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your account and profile
- Your recipes
- Your collections
- Your likes and saves
- Your follow relationships
